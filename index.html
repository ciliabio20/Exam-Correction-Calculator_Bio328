How the maximum bonus is calculated
Original score 0–54%: maximum bonus = 15 points
Original score above 54–69%: maximum bonus = 15 − 0.8 × (original score − 54)
Original score above 69–96%: maximum bonus = 3 − (2.8 ÷ 27) × (original score − 69)
Original score above 96–100%: maximum bonus = 0.05 × (100 − original score)

Revised grade = original score + maximum bonus × (correction credit ÷ 100)

Important: A 54% original score can rise to at most 69% from written corrections under this scale. Any separate opportunity to reach 70% through a mastery assessment is not included in this calculator. Bonus points are earned based on the quality of completed corrections.
